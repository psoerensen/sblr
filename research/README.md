# Research archive

This tree contains non-production method research and historical scientific
snapshots. It is excluded from the package build and does not define public
interfaces, sampler defaults, or benchmark evidence.

- [`sbayesrc/`](sbayesrc/) preserves investigations of annotation-dependent
  marker mixture membership, including the continuous-alpha development
  endpoint and supporting Study 06 investigations.
- [`sbayesrv/`](sbayesrv/) is the research home for annotation-dependent
  effect variance within non-null BayesR components.

Accepted benchmark evidence remains in `sblrbench/results/reference/`.
