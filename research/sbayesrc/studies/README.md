thresholds, conclusions, and provenance from supporting SBayesRC investigations
formerly stored under `sblrbench/studies/06_annotation_models/`.

`sblrbench/results/reference/06_annotation_models/`. The snapshots here are
local or research-only support and are not package tests, public APIs, current
benchmark entries, or instructions to regenerate evidence. Historical paths
inside preserved files describe their original execution context.
