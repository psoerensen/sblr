# Study 12 Aspect 2 — decision-emission addendum

Recorded after compact result extraction on 2026-08-10. This addendum does
not change the preregistered hypotheses, thresholds, model fits, or endpoint
definitions.

The Outcome A--G statements in `design_aspect2.md` are scientifically
nonexclusive. The first implementation of the compact decision emitter used
an `else if` priority and therefore returned Outcome C alone even when the
already-frozen screens also supported Outcomes E and F. The emitter is amended
to report every applicable registered outcome. In this realization:

- C applies because RC is within the registered 0.01 downstream-correlation
  equivalence tolerance of LV;
- E applies because ordinary R is also within that tolerance of LV; and
- F applies because all theta truth values lie in their intervals while q
  misses the preregistered truth-recovery screen.

This is a reporting correction for overlapping preregistered interpretations,
not a post-result change to their scientific criteria.
