# SBayesRV research

SBayesRV is the current research name for models in which annotations or
derived signals modify effect variance within non-null BayesR components. The
reserved future method identifier is `"sbayesrv"`.

This directory is research-only. It does not introduce a public
`method = "sbayesrv"` interface, a new sampler, or a promoted benchmark. The
historical Study 12 material is preserved under [`prior_study12/`](prior_study12/)
as local-only, unpromoted evidence that is paused pending redesign.

Historical documents may retain the former SBayesR-LV wording for traceability.
New work should use SBayesRV and keep marker-variance modulation distinct from
SBayesRC's annotation-dependent mixture membership.
